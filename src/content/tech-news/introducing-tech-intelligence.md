Technology news moves quickly, but a long list of announcements rarely helps developers decide what deserves attention.

Tech Intelligence is a new Surya Tech Codex briefing for meaningful updates across AI, .NET, Azure, cloud platforms, and developer tools. Each update will explain what changed, why it matters, who should care, and what to explore next.

## What each update will include

- a concise summary of the announcement;
- practical impact for developers and architects;
- important limitations or adoption considerations;
- links to primary, official sources; and
- a clear takeaway that helps readers decide whether to act.

## How the briefing will stay useful

The feed will favour meaningful releases and engineering decisions over volume. Updates will be organised by topic and date so readers can scan quickly, open the items relevant to their work, and return to the original source when they need the full announcement.

The first technology briefings will appear here as they are reviewed.
